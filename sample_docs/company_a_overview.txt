Aerofield Dynamics — Company Overview (fictional, for demo purposes)

Aerofield Dynamics is a mid-sized aerospace components manufacturer founded in 2011,
specializing in structural fasteners and precision-machined brackets for commercial
aircraft. The company operates three manufacturing facilities and reported revenue
of $180 million in its most recent fiscal year, up 9% year over year.

Strategy: Aerofield has focused on vertical integration, acquiring two raw-material
suppliers in the past three years to reduce input-cost volatility. Management has
stated a goal of reducing average order lead time from 45 days to 30 days by the
end of next fiscal year.

Risks: The company disclosed reliance on a single titanium supplier for roughly 40%
of raw material volume, and noted rising labor costs at its primary facility as a
margin pressure point. Customer concentration is moderate, with its top three
customers accounting for 55% of revenue.

Recent developments: Aerofield announced a partnership with a regional logistics
provider to improve on-time delivery performance, targeting a 95% on-time rate
within 18 months, up from a reported 82%.
