Vantage Precision Corp — Company Overview (fictional, for demo purposes)

Vantage Precision Corp is a diversified precision-manufacturing company serving the
aerospace, defense, and medical device industries. Founded in 1998, the company
reported revenue of $310 million last fiscal year, roughly flat versus the prior
year, with management citing softness in its medical device segment.

Strategy: Vantage has pursued diversification across end markets rather than
vertical integration, arguing this reduces exposure to any single industry's
cycle. The company completed one acquisition in the defense-electronics space
during the past year, its first acquisition in over five years.

Risks: Management flagged increased competitive pricing pressure in the medical
device segment and a multi-year backlog of aging equipment in one facility that
will require capital investment. Customer concentration is low, with no single
customer exceeding 12% of revenue.

Recent developments: Vantage disclosed an ongoing ERP system migration expected to
be substantially complete within two fiscal years, intended to unify reporting
across its business units, which currently operate on separate systems inherited
through prior acquisitions.
